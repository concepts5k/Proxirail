PET Way

Post-Encroachment Time (PET) is the time gap between one road user leaving a shared conflict zone and another entering it — here, between a pedestrian and a train at a grade crossing. Shorter PET values indicate closer calls and higher risk. Select a railroad ID below to view its PET information and crossing feed.
